# Planner pieces

`Thread` (the transcript on the route line, with Sara's one-time thinking bar), `Composer`,
`TurnFailure` (why Sara did not answer, and a real retry), `Understood` (from, to, arrive by and
the flight, or "Sara will ask"), `PlanResult` and `ItineraryRow` (leave, arrive, margin,
transfers, walking and the public fare, each time with how it is known) and `StartTrip`.
`CityAnswers` shows what Sara found about the city under her words: the subject and its verdict,
the details on the route line with their times, and each source with the time of its data, or
why it could not be read.
